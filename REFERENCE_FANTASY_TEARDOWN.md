# fantasy.co — concise experience teardown

## What is being emulated
Not the brand or copy. The target is the **type of experience**: a high-drama consulting / innovation landing page that behaves like an editorial film trailer more than a normal service site.

## Layer breakdown

### 1. Hero behavior
- Full-screen opening scene.
- Minimal chrome, lots of negative space, oversized headline.
- Hero is not a sales block; it is a **positioning statement**.
- Supporting info is split into small modules around the dominant headline rather than stacked under it.

### 2. Sequence of sections
1. Full-screen hero / statement
2. Short services trio with numeric framing (01 / 02 / 03)
3. Large trust / positioning statement
4. Sticky media-gallery section with immersive visual dominance
5. Deeper card-based narrative / cases
6. Expansive closing section with contact CTA

### 3. Pacing
- Starts slow and monumental.
- Speeds up in the middle through multiple visual beats.
- Holds attention with sticky sections and reveal-like scroll progression.
- Ends with a calmer but still premium CTA.

### 4. Typography rhythm
- Very large display headlines.
- Short supporting sentences.
- Strong contrast between giant statements and small utility labels.
- Numbering and micro-labels create editorial cadence.

### 5. Media usage
- Motion-heavy media dominates the middle of the page.
- Text-to-visual ratio is low in hero and gallery areas, higher in case / proof sections.
- Visual blocks are used as pacing devices, not decoration.

### 6. Animation density
- Medium-high density.
- Not playful micro-interactions everywhere; more scroll-led atmosphere.
- Continuous ambient movement, subtle transforms, sticky pinning, fades, scale shifts.

### 7. Transitions
- Section-to-section transitions rely on contrast in density: sparse -> dense -> sparse.
- Visual layers overlap with gradients, masks, and depth.
- Sticky behavior creates the feeling of scenes replacing one another.

### 8. Interaction grammar
- Scroll is the primary interaction.
- Navigation is light and secondary.
- Cards and panels feel cinematic/editorial rather than app-like.
- Calls to action are delayed until trust and atmosphere are established.

## Build translation rules for this remake
- Match **dramaturgy**, not surface wording.
- Keep a **similar screen count and tempo**.
- Preserve the **visual-first mid-page sequence**.
- Use Russian-language AI/automation messaging only after the structure is established.
